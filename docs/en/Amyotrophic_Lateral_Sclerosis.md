# 📉 Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)

**Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS, Charcot's Disease, Lou Gehrig's Disease)** is a rapidly progressive and invariably fatal neurodegenerative disorder characterized by the simultaneous degeneration of **upper motor neurons (UMN)** in the primary motor cortex and **lower motor neurons (LMN)** in the brainstem and anterior horns of the spinal cord.

---

## 🔬 Molecular Pathophysiology

1. **TDP-43 Proteinopathy**:
   - In >95% of all ALS cases, nuclear TAR DNA-binding protein 43 (TDP-43) mislocalizes to the cytoplasm, forming ubiquitinated, hyperphosphorylated neurotoxic aggregates.
2. **Genetic Mutations**:
   - **C9orf72**: Hexanucleotide GGGGCC repeat expansion (most frequent cause of familial and sporadic ALS, shared with [[Frontotemporal_Dementia|FTD]]).
   - **SOD1**: Superoxide dismutase 1 conformational mutants triggering toxic gain-of-function.
   - **TARDBP** and **FUS** mutations.
3. **Glutamate Excitotoxicity**:
   - Astrocytic EAAT2 transporter loss causes chronic synaptic glutamate accumulation and lethal calcium influx.

---

## 🩺 Clinical Phenotype

### Upper Motor Neuron (UMN) Signs:
- Spasticity, hyperreflexia, clonus.
- Pseudobulbar affect (pathological laughter/crying).
- Positive Babinski and Hoffmann reflexes.

### Lower Motor Neuron (LMN) Signs:
- Progressive muscle weakness, amyotrophy (wasting).
- Coarse fasciculations (spontaneous muscle twitches).
- Flaccidity and hypo/areflexia.

### Clinical Presentation Modes:
- **Spinal Onset (~70%)**: Asymmetric distal limb weakness (e.g., foot drop, loss of manual dexterity).
- **Bulbar Onset (~25%)**: Dysarthria (slurred, spastic/flaccid speech), dysphagia, tongue atrophy, and fasciculations.

## 📋 Functional Monitoring: ALSFRS-R
From [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales]]:
- **ALSFRS-R (Revised ALS Functional Rating Scale)**:
  - 12 items scored 0 to 4 (maximum total score 48).
  - Domains: Bulbar (speech, salivation, swallowing), Fine Motor (handwriting, cutting food), Gross Motor (walking, climbing stairs), and Respiratory (orthopnea, dyspnea, ventilation).
  - The rate of score decline (ΔFS) is the primary clinical prognostic parameter.

---

## 🧲 Diagnostic Modalities
- **Electromyography (Needle EMG)**: Demonstration of active denervation (fibrillation potentials, positive sharp waves) and chronic reinnervation across multiple spinal/bulbar regions (Gold Coast Criteria).
- **MRI Findings** (see [[MRI_Biomarkers_and_Neuroimaging]]):
  - Precentral gyrus motor cortex atrophy.
  - T2/FLAIR hyperintensity along the corticospinal tracts.
  - "Motor band sign" (hypointense rim in motor cortex on T2*/SWI).
- **CSF & Blood Biomarkers** (see [[Laboratory_and_CSF_Biomarkers]]):
  - Massive elevation of **Neurofilament Light (NfL)** (often 10–20x above normal limits).
