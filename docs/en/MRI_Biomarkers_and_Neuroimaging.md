# 🧲 MRI Biomarkers and Quantitative Neuroimaging

High-field Magnetic Resonance Imaging (1.5T, 3.0T, 7.0T) provides in-vivo visualization of central nervous system anatomy, atrophy trajectories, and focal inflammatory lesions.

---

## 📡 Primary Pulse Sequences

1. **T1-Weighted 3D Imaging (T1-WI)**:
   - High spatial resolution and sharp tissue contrast between gray and white matter.
   - Foundation for 3D automated volumetric segmentation (hippocampus, ventricles, whole brain).
   - T1 gadolinium-enhanced imaging reveals acute blood-brain barrier disruption.
2. **T2-WI and T2-FLAIR (Fluid-Attenuated Inversion Recovery)**:
   - Free water (CSF) suppression allows hyperintense demyelinating plaques and vasogenic edema to stand out clearly.
   - Primary sequence for McDonald criteria evaluation in [[Multiple_Sclerosis|Multiple Sclerosis]].
3. **SWI / T2* (Susceptibility-Weighted Imaging)**:
   - Sensitive to paramagnetic compounds (iron, hemosiderin, deoxygenated blood).
   - Visualizes the loss of the swallow-tail sign in [[Parkinsons_Disease|Parkinson's Disease]] and the motor band sign in [[Amyotrophic_Lateral_Sclerosis|ALS]].
4. **Diffusion-Weighted Imaging (DWI / ADC)**:
   - Quantifies Brownian motion of water molecules, identifying cytotoxic edema in ischemia and encephalitis.

---

## 📊 Quantitative Volumetric Parameters

### 1. Hippocampal Volume (mm³)
- Measured bilaterally. Normal adult range: **3800–4400 mm³**.
- Significant volume reduction (<3000 mm³) is a hallmark biomarker of [[Alzheimers_Disease|Alzheimer's Disease]] and MCI.

### 2. Normalized Whole Brain Volume (nWBV)
- Ratio of brain parenchymal volume to total intracranial volume (ICV).
- Normal range: **0.76–0.82**.
- Decreases continuously with global cerebral atrophy.

### 3. Ventricular-to-Brain Ratio (VBR)
- Ratio of lateral ventricular volume to brain parenchyma.
- Rises markedly in ex-vacuo hydrocephalus associated with neurodegeneration.

### 4. T2 Lesion Burden (Count and Volume)
- Quantifies periventricular, juxtacortical, infratentorial, and spinal cord white matter hyperintensities in [[Multiple_Sclerosis|Multiple Sclerosis]].

---

## 🗺️ Key Radiological Signatures

- **Dawson's Fingers**: Ovoid T2/FLAIR lesions oriented perpendicular to the lateral ventricles along deep medullary venules in [[Multiple_Sclerosis|Multiple Sclerosis]].
- **Swallow-Tail Sign Loss**: Absence of the hyperintense nigrosome-1 pocket within the substantia nigra on high-resolution axial SWI in [[Parkinsons_Disease|Parkinson's Disease]].
- **Motor Band Sign**: Hypointense precentral gyrus ribbon on T2*/SWI reflecting iron accumulation in [[Amyotrophic_Lateral_Sclerosis|ALS]].
- **LETM**: Longitudinally extensive transverse myelitis spanning ≥ 3 contiguous vertebrae in [[Neuromyelitis_Optica_NMOSD|NMOSD]].
