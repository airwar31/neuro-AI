# ⚡ Parkinson's Disease (PD)

**Parkinson's Disease** is the second most common neurodegenerative disorder, caused by the selective and progressive degeneration of dopaminergic neurons in the **substantia nigra pars compacta (SNpc)** and the accumulation of intraneuronal inclusions called **Lewy bodies** containing aggregated alpha-synuclein.

---

## 🔬 Pathophysiology

1. **Nigrostriatal Denervation**:
   - Loss of dopaminergic projection fibers to the putamen and caudate nucleus.
   - Disinhibition of the indirect pathway and overactivation of the subthalamic nucleus (STN) and globus pallidus internus (GPi), causing excessive inhibitory outflow to the thalamocortical motor circuit.
2. **Alpha-Synuclein Aggregation**:
   - Misfolded α-synuclein propagates in a prion-like stereotypic manner through neural connectomes (Braak staging: enteric nervous system and olfactory bulb -> brainstem -> substantia nigra -> neocortex).

---

## 🏃 Clinical Manifestations

### Classic Motor Tetrad:
1. **Bradykinesia**: Slowness of movement initiation and progressive decrement in speed/amplitude during repetitive finger/foot tapping.
2. **Resting Tremor**: Low-frequency (4–6 Hz) "pill-rolling" tremor that attenuates with purposeful voluntary action.
3. **Lead-Pipe / Cogwheel Rigidity**: Constant, uniform resistance to passive joint movement across agonist and antagonist muscle groups.
4. **Postural Instability**: Impaired righting reflexes (tested via the pull test), predisposing to falls in advanced stages.

### Non-Motor Prodromes (occurring 5–15 years prior to motor onset):
- REM sleep behavior disorder (RBD: dream enactment, flailing during sleep).
- Hyposmia / anosmia (loss of smell).
- Severe autonomic dysfunction (chronic constipation, orthostatic hypotension).
- Mood disorders (anxiety, apathy, depression).

## 📋 Clinical Rating Scales
From [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales]]:
- **MDS-UPDRS (Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale)**:
  - Part III (Motor Examination): 33 scores covering speech, facial expression, tremor, rigidity, gait, and agility (0–132 points).
- **Hoehn and Yahr Staging**: Stages 1 (unilateral) to 5 (wheelchair/bedbound).

## 🧲 Neuroimaging Biomarkers
From [[MRI_Biomarkers_and_Neuroimaging]]:
- **Loss of the "Swallow-Tail" Sign**: High-resolution SWI/T2* demonstrates loss of the hyperintense nigrosome-1 signal in the dorsolateral substantia nigra due to iron deposition and neuronal loss.
- **Neuromelanin-sensitive MRI**: Direct visualization of signal attenuation in the substantia nigra.
- **DaTscan (123I-Ioflupane SPECT)**: Demonstrates asymmetrical loss of striatal dopamine transporters (putaminal uptake deficit).
