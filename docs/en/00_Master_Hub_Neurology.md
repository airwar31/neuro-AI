# 🧠 Master Hub: Neurodegenerative and Autoimmune Diseases

Welcome to the central knowledge hub of **NeuroAI**. This knowledge base is structured as a bidirectional graph of interconnected concepts, clinical assessment scales, radiological biomarkers, and multimodal machine learning models.

---

## 📌 Knowledge Graph Architecture

### 1. [[Neurodegenerative_Diseases|Neurodegenerative Diseases]]
Progressive loss of neuronal structure and synaptic integrity driven by abnormal protein aggregates (proteopathies):
- 🧬 [[Alzheimers_Disease]] — Amyloid-β (Aβ42) and tau pathology, primary cause of dementia worldwide.
- ⚡ [[Parkinsons_Disease]] — Synucleinopathy, nigrostriatal dopaminergic loss, and classical motor triad.
- 📉 [[Amyotrophic_Lateral_Sclerosis]] — Dual upper and lower motor neuron degeneration, TDP-43 pathology.
- 🎭 [[Frontotemporal_Dementia]] — Behavioral changes and primary progressive aphasias (PPA).
- 🧬 [[Huntingtons_Disease]] — Monogenic trinucleotide CAG repeat expansion in the HTT gene, chorea, and subcortical dementia.

### 2. [[Autoimmune_CNS_Disorders|Autoimmune CNS Disorders]]
Immune-mediated injury to neural tissue featuring blood-brain barrier disruption, demyelination, and pathogenic autoantibodies:
- 🛡️ [[Multiple_Sclerosis]] — Chronic inflammatory demyelinating disease of the brain and spinal cord.
- 👁️ [[Neuromyelitis_Optica_NMOSD]] (Devic's Disease) — Severe astrocytopathy with autoantibodies to Aquaporin-4 (AQP4-IgG).
- 🔥 [[Autoimmune_Encephalitis]] — Acute-onset psychosis, seizures, and dyskinesias (Anti-NMDAR, LGI1, CASPR2).
- ⚡ [[Myasthenia_Gravis]] — Autoimmune blockade of the neuromuscular junction (anti-AChR and anti-MuSK antibodies).
- 🚶 [[Guillain_Barre_Syndrome]] — Acute post-infectious inflammatory demyelinating polyradiculoneuropathy.

### 3. Diagnostic Modalities and Multimodal Machine Learning
- 📋 [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales]] — Standardized instruments: MMSE, MoCA, UPDRS, EDSS, ALSFRS-R, and FSS.
- 🧲 [[MRI_Biomarkers_and_Neuroimaging]] — T1 volumetry, hippocampal volumetry, ventricular-brain ratio (VBR), T2/FLAIR lesions, McDonald criteria.
- 🧪 [[Laboratory_and_CSF_Biomarkers]] — Cerebrospinal fluid oligoclonal IgG bands, Neurofilament Light chain (NfL), Aβ42/tau ratios.
- 🤖 [[Multimodal_AI_in_Neurology]] — Deep fusion architecture combining subjective survey responses with objective quantitative neuroimaging.
