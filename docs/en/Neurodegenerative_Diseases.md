# 🧬 Neurodegenerative Diseases of the Central Nervous System

**Neurodegenerative diseases** represent a diverse group of chronic, incurable disorders characterized by progressive loss of selectively vulnerable populations of neurons, breakdown of synaptic networks, and accumulation of misfolded toxic protein aggregates.

---

## 🔬 Core Pathophysiological Mechanisms

1. **Proteopathies and Protein Misfolding**:
   - Failure of protein quality control systems (ubiquitin-proteasome system and autophagy-lysosome pathway).
   - Insoluble β-sheet fibril deposition:
     - Amyloid-β plaques and hyperphosphorylated tau neurofibrillary tangles in [[Alzheimers_Disease|Alzheimer's Disease]].
     - Alpha-synuclein aggregates (Lewy bodies) in [[Parkinsons_Disease|Parkinson's Disease]].
     - Cytoplasmic TDP-43 inclusions in [[Amyotrophic_Lateral_Sclerosis|ALS]] and [[Frontotemporal_Dementia|FTD]].
     - Polyglutamine-expanded huntingtin in [[Huntingtons_Disease|Huntington's Disease]].

2. **Mitochondrial Dysfunction and Oxidative Stress**:
   - Impairment of electron transport chain complex I/IV, reactive oxygen species (ROS) overproduction, and lipid peroxidation.

3. **Excitotoxicity**:
   - Defective astrocytic glutamate reuptake (EAAT2 downregulation), persistent NMDA receptor hyperactivation, excessive intracellular Ca²⁺ influx, and apoptotic protease activation.

4. **Chronic Neuroinflammation**:
   - Microglial priming and reactive A1 neurotoxic astrogliosis releasing TNF-α, IL-1β, and nitric oxide.
