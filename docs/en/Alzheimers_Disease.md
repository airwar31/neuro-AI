# 🧠 Alzheimer's Disease (AD)

**Alzheimer's Disease** is the most prevalent neurodegenerative dementia worldwide, responsible for 60–80% of all progressive cognitive impairments in the elderly. It is characterized by progressive memory loss, spatial disorientation, executive dysfunction, and eventual loss of functional independence.

---

## 🧬 Pathophysiological Cascades

1. **Amyloid-β Plaque Formation**:
   - Amyloid precursor protein (APP) undergoes sequential cleavage by β-secretase (BACE1) and γ-secretase complex.
   - Generates the hydrophobic **Aβ42** peptide, which self-assembles into oligomers, synaptic disruptors, and extracellular amyloid plaques.
2. **Tau Hyperphosphorylation and Tangles**:
   - Microtubule-associated protein tau becomes hyperphosphorylated, detaches from axonal microtubules, and forms toxic paired helical filaments and intraneuronal **neurofibrillary tangles (NFTs)**.
3. **Cholinergic Deficit**:
   - Degeneration of cholinergic neurons in the basal forebrain (nucleus basalis of Meynert), resulting in profound cortical acetylcholine deficiency.

---

## 📈 Clinical Stages

1. **Preclinical AD**:
   - Clinically asymptomatic with positive biomarker evidence (decreased CSF Aβ42, elevated plasma p-tau217).
2. **Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI due to AD)**:
   - Objective episodic memory impairment with preserved activities of daily living (ADLs).
   - [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales#MoCA|MoCA]] score: 20–25; [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales#MMSE|MMSE]] score: 24–27.
3. **Mild to Moderate Dementia**:
   - Marked anterograde amnesia, dyspraxia, topographical disorientation, loss of financial management skills.
   - [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales#MMSE|MMSE]] score: 18–23.
4. **Severe Dementia**:
   - Global cognitive collapse, mutism, dysphagia, complete nursing dependence.
   - [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales#MMSE|MMSE]] score: < 12.

## 🧲 Neuroimaging Markers
According to [[MRI_Biomarkers_and_Neuroimaging]]:
- **Hippocampal Volumetry**: Marked bilateral volume reduction (< 3000 mm³ vs age-matched normal > 4000 mm³).
- **Medial Temporal Atrophy (MTA / Scheltens Scale)**: Graded 0 to 4 based on width of the choroid fissure, temporal horn dilation, and hippocampal height.
- **Ventricular Enlargement (VBR)**: Compensatory lateral ventriculomegaly (VBR > 0.08).
- **Normalized Whole Brain Volume (nWBV)**: Diffuse parenchymal atrophy below 0.70.

---

## 🧪 Laboratory Biomarkers
In accordance with [[Laboratory_and_CSF_Biomarkers]]:
- Decreased **Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio** in CSF and blood plasma.
- Elevated **p-tau181** and **p-tau217** levels.
- Elevated **Neurofilament Light (NfL)** marking ongoing neuroaxonal breakdown.
