# 🧪 Laboratory and CSF Biomarkers

Analysis of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) obtained via lumbar puncture, complemented by ultra-sensitive blood-based biomarker assays (e.g., Simoa digital immunoassay), delivers objective molecular verification of neuroinflammation, demyelination, and axonal death.

---

## 🛡️ Neuroinflammatory and Demyelinating Markers

### 1. Oligoclonal IgG Bands (OCB)
- **Methodology**: Isoelectric focusing (IEF) of paired CSF and serum followed by immunoblotting.
- **Patterns**:
  - *Pattern 1*: Normal (no bands detected in CSF or serum).
  - *Pattern 2*: **Intrathecal IgG synthesis** (bands present exclusively in CSF) — classic biomarker of [[Multiple_Sclerosis|Multiple Sclerosis]] (>95% sensitivity).
  - *Pattern 3*: Intrathecal and systemic synthesis.
  - *Pattern 4*: Passive transudation due to impaired blood-brain barrier permeability ("mirror pattern").

### 2. Autoantibodies
- **AQP4-IgG**: Serum biomarker identifying astrocytopathy in [[Neuromyelitis_Optica_NMOSD|NMOSD]].
- **MOG-IgG**: Biomarker for Myelin Oligodendrocyte Glycoprotein Antibody-associated Disease (MOGAD).
- **Anti-NMDAR, Anti-LGI1**: Verified in CSF and serum for [[Autoimmune_Encephalitis|Autoimmune Encephalitis]].
- **AChR and MuSK Autoantibodies**: Diagnostic confirmation of [[Myasthenia_Gravis|Myasthenia Gravis]].

## 🧬 Neurodegeneration and Axonal Injury Biomarkers

### 1. Neurofilament Light Chain (NfL)
- Structural scaffolding protein of the axonal cytoskeleton. Released into CSF and blood following neuroaxonal damage.
- **Clinical Utility**:
  - Extreme 10–20x elevation in [[Amyotrophic_Lateral_Sclerosis|ALS]].
  - Correlates with active relapse rate and subclinical disease activity in [[Multiple_Sclerosis|MS]].
  - Tracks neurodegenerative rate in [[Alzheimers_Disease|Alzheimer's Disease]].

### 2. Alzheimer's Disease Fluid Biomarkers
- **CSF Aβ42 and Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio**: Marked decline reflects cortical amyloid sequestration in [[Alzheimers_Disease|Alzheimer's Disease]].
- **Phosphorylated Tau (p-tau181, p-tau217)**: High correlation with neurofibrillary tangle burden.
- **Total Tau (t-tau)**: Reflects acute and subacute cortical neurodegeneration.
