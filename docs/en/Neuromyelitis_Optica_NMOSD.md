# 👁️ Neuromyelitis Optica Spectrum Disorder (NMOSD / Devic's Disease)

**Neuromyelitis Optica Spectrum Disorder (NMOSD)** is an aggressive, complement-mediated autoimmune astrocytopathy characterized by recurrent attacks of severe optic neuritis and longitudinally extensive transverse myelitis. It is mechanistically and therapeutically distinct from [[Multiple_Sclerosis|Multiple Sclerosis]].

---

## 🔬 Pathophysiology
- Autoantibodies targeting **Aquaporin-4 (AQP4-IgG)** bind to water channel tetramers on astrocyte foot processes at the blood-brain barrier.
- Fixation of C1q triggers the classical complement cascade, membrane attack complex (MAC) assembly, astrocyte lysis, secondary oligodendrocyte death, and tissue necrosis.

---

## 🩺 Core Clinical Manifestations
1. **Optic Neuritis**:
   - Severe, often bilateral visual loss, optic chiasm involvement, poor recovery.
2. **Longitudinally Extensive Transverse Myelitis (LETM)**:
   - Spinal cord MRI lesion extending continuously over **≥ 3 contiguous vertebral segments**, occupying >50% of the cross-sectional area with central necrosis.
3. **Area Postrema Syndrome**:
   - Intractable hiccups, nausea, and vomiting unresponsive to standard antiemetics (dorsal medulla involvement).
4. Acute brainstem syndrome, symptomatic narcolepsy, and diencephalic lesions.

---

## ⚖️ Differential Diagnosis: MS vs NMOSD

| Feature | Multiple Sclerosis ([[Multiple_Sclerosis\|MS]]) | NMOSD (Devic's Disease) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Cellular Target** | Oligodendrocyte / Myelin | Astrocyte (AQP4 water channel) |
| **Spinal Cord Lesion** | Short (< 2 segments), eccentric | Longitudinally extensive (LETM ≥ 3 segments), central |
| **Brain MRI** | Periventricular Dawson's fingers | Usually normal or periependymal non-specific lesions |
| **CSF Oligoclonal Bands** | Present in >95% (Type 2) | Typically absent (<20%) |
| **Autoantibodies** | Absent | **AQP4-IgG** (>99% specific) |
| **First-Line Relapse Therapy** | Methylprednisolone | Urgent **Therapeutic Plasma Exchange (PLEX)** |
