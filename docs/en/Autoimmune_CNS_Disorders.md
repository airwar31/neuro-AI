# 🛡️ Autoimmune and Neuroimmunological Disorders

**Autoimmune CNS Disorders** comprise diseases where self-tolerance is broken and the adaptive immune system (autoreactive CD4+ and CD8+ T lymphocytes, B cell clones, autoantibodies, and the complement cascade) invades across the blood-brain barrier (BBB) to target myelin, neuronal ion channels, or glial cells.

---

## 🔬 Central Immunological Concepts

1. **Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB) Diapedesis**:
   - Integrins (such as α4β1 / VLA-4) on activated T cells bind endothelial adhesion molecules (VCAM-1), enabling transmigration into the Virchow-Robin perivascular spaces.
2. **Inflammatory Demyelination**:
   - Macrophage and microglial stripping of the myelin sheath, leading to conduction slowing and saltatory conduction failure.
3. **Astrocytopathy vs Myelinopathy**:
   - Multiple Sclerosis primarily affects oligodendrocytes and myelin sheaths.
   - NMOSD directly destroys astrocytes via anti-AQP4 autoantibodies and complement-mediated cytotoxicity.

## 📊 Autoimmune Diagnostic Comparison

| Disorder | Autoimmune Target | Hallmarks | MRI Pattern | Characteristic Biomarker |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [[Multiple_Sclerosis]] | Myelin proteins (MBP, MOG) | Relapsing-remitting neurological attacks | Periventricular Dawson's fingers, DIS/DIT | CSF Oligoclonal Bands (Type 2) |
| [[Neuromyelitis_Optica_NMOSD]] | Aquaporin-4 (AQP4) water channels | Bilateral optic neuritis, severe myelitis | Longitudinal transverse myelitis (LETM ≥3 segments) | Serum AQP4-IgG (>99% specific) |
| [[Autoimmune_Encephalitis]] | Neuronal receptors (NMDAR, LGI1) | Acute psychosis, seizures, dyskinesias | Medial temporal / limbic T2 hyperintensity | CSF receptor autoantibodies |
| [[Myasthenia_Gravis]] | Neuromuscular junction (AChR, MuSK) | Fatigable ptosis, diplopia, weakness | Normal (Chest CT: Thymoma) | Anti-AChR / anti-MuSK antibodies |
| [[Guillain_Barre_Syndrome]] | Peripheral nerve gangliosides | Rapidly ascending flaccid tetraparesis | Spinal root enhancement | Albuminocytologic dissociation |
