# 🛡️ Multiple Sclerosis (MS)

**Multiple Sclerosis** is a chronic, immune-mediated demyelinating and neurodegenerative disease of the central nervous system, representing the leading cause of non-traumatic neurological disability in young adults.

---

## 🧬 Phenotypic Disease Courses

1. **Clinically Isolated Syndrome (CIS)**:
   - A single monomodal or multimodal episode of neurological dysfunction lasting at least 24 hours.
2. **Relapsing-Remitting MS (RRMS)**:
   - Represents ~85% of initial presentations. Characterized by clearly defined clinical attacks (relapses) followed by complete or partial remission.
3. **Secondary Progressive MS (SPMS)**:
   - Gradual, non-relapse-dependent accumulation of neurological disability following an initial RRMS phase.
4. **Primary Progressive MS (PPMS)**:
   - Continuous worsening of neurological function from disease onset (~10–15% of patients), predominantly manifesting as progressive spastic paraparesis.
5. **Radiologically Isolated Syndrome (RIS)**:
   - Incidental discovery of typical MS lesions on MRI in individuals lacking clinical neurological signs.

---

## 🧲 McDonald 2017 Diagnostic Criteria and MRI
Definitive diagnosis requires demonstrating **Dissemination in Space (DIS)** and **Dissemination in Time (DIT)**. Detailed in [[MRI_Biomarkers_and_Neuroimaging]]:
- **Dissemination in Space (DIS)**: ≥1 T2-hyperintense lesion in at least 2 of 4 CNS locations:
  1. *Periventricular* (Dawson's fingers radiating perpendicularly from ventricles).
  2. *Cortical / Juxtacortical* (touching the cerebral cortex).
  3. *Infratentorial* (brainstem, cerebellar peduncles).
  4. *Spinal Cord* (cervical/thoracic lesions < 2 vertebral bodies in length).
- **Dissemination in Time (DIT)**:
  - Simultaneous presence of asymptomatic gadolinium-enhancing and non-enhancing lesions on a single scan, OR a new T2/gadolinium-enhancing lesion on follow-up MRI.
  - **Presence of CSF-specific oligoclonal bands fulfills the DIT requirement!**

## 📋 Disability Quantitation: The EDSS Scale
From [[Clinical_Questionnaires_and_Scales]]:
- **EDSS (Expanded Disability Status Scale)**:
  - `0.0–3.5`: Fully ambulatory, mild to moderate functional system impairments.
  - `4.0–4.5`: Fully ambulatory without aid, severe disability, walking distance limited to 500/300 meters.
  - `6.0`: Intermittent or unilateral assistance required to walk 100 meters (cane/crutch).
  - `6.5`: Constant bilateral assistance required to walk 20 meters.
  - `7.0+`: Wheelchair confined.
- **Fatigue Severity Scale (FSS)**: High prevalence (>80%) of disabling central fatigue.

## 🧪 Laboratory Biomarkers
From [[Laboratory_and_CSF_Biomarkers]]:
- **Oligoclonal IgG Bands (OCB)**: Isoelectric focusing demonstrates CSF-restricted Type 2 oligoclonal synthesis in >95% of MS patients.
- **Serum/CSF NfL**: Quantitative surrogate of ongoing acute axonal injury and treatment response.
